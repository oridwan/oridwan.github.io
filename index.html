npj Comput. Mater. 2026 · Mach. Learn.: Sci. Technol. 2026

LEGO-xtal: symmetry-aware generative crystal design

Generative models learn crystals as space-group and Wyckoff-site tables, and an SO(3)-descriptor optimizer steers each candidate toward a target local environment before ranking with MACE and DFT. A differentiable GPU version refines structures in both direct and latent space.

  • 1,741 low-energy sp² carbon allotropes generated from 25 known seeds.
  • Four new negative-curvature graphites; two lie below the previous lowest-energy structure, and phonons confirm dynamical stability.
  • ~5× faster on GPU than the CPU workflow, with ~2.5× more unique low-energy structures.

Rare-earth magnets · DOE ARPA-E MAGNITO · 2026

CSPFlow: automated crystal structure prediction from AI generation to DFT

An end-to-end pipeline that generates candidates with a diffusion model, screens them with ML interatomic potentials, places the survivors on a convex hull against Materials Project references, and streams the best through spin-polarized DFT on SLURM clusters.

  • Rare-earth 4f electrons are kept in the core for reliable energetics, and saturation magnetization is reconstructed from Hund's rules.
  • Each campaign lives in one resumable database: inexpensive ML screening runs to completion, and DFT is streamed under a core budget.
  • 1,200+ automated tests and a 13-chapter user guide.

MMI Data Hub: an open database of computed crystal structures

I created and lead the lab's public data platform. Results are searchable by formula, chemical system, element or space group, with structure viewers, downloadable DFT inputs and citation metadata for archiving.

  • 17,596 structures across five databases: carbon allotropes, rare-earth magnets, electrides, energetic materials and PXRD solutions.
  • 86 elements represented.

Representation learning · arXiv 2026

Crystal representation in reciprocal space

Common crystal representations are not one-to-one, which complicates structure matching and generative-model training. This work maps crystals to a 4D reciprocal space of Cartesian coordinates and scattering factors and converts it to a rotation-invariant power spectrum.

  • Invariant to translation, rotation and choice of crystallographic setting.
  • Demonstrated on structure matching and reconstruction tasks.
