Generative AI · npj Comput. Mater. 2026 · MLST 2026

LEGO-xtal: symmetry-aware generative crystal design

Generative models learn crystals as space-group and Wyckoff-site tables. An SO(3)-descriptor optimizer then steers each candidate toward a target local environment before energy ranking with MACE and DFT. The GPU version makes the pipeline fully differentiable and alternates refinement in direct and latent space.

Automated discovery · Rare-earth magnets · DOE ARPA-E MAGNITO

CSPFlow: automated crystal structure prediction, from AI generation to DFT

A fully automated pipeline that generates candidates with a diffusion model, screens them with ML interatomic potentials, places the survivors on a convex hull, and streams the best through spin-polarized DFT. Built for rare-earth transition-metal magnet searches, it treats the 4f electrons in the core for reliable energetics and reconstructs saturation magnetization with Hund's rules. It runs unattended on SLURM clusters.

Representation learning · Preprint 2026

Crystal representation in reciprocal space

Crystal representations used in practice are not one-to-one, which hurts structure matching and generative-model training. This work maps crystals to a 4D reciprocal space of Cartesian coordinates and scattering factors and converts it to a rotation-invariant power spectrum. The result is a continuous, symmetry-aware representation of atomic positions and lattice parameters.
