Research

Selected projects

Flagship · npj Computational Materials 2026

LEGO-xtal: symmetry-aware generative design of crystals

A generative framework that builds crystals from symmetry-informed building blocks and steers generation toward a target local chemical environment. Developed with UNC Charlotte Computer Science and the Université de Poitiers computational chemistry group.

  • Expanded 25 known low-energy sp² carbon allotropes into 1,700+ new candidates within 0.5 eV/atom of graphite.
  • Generation respects space-group constraints and periodicity by construction.
  • Open-source and fully reproducible.
Read the paper Code on GitHub
25 → 1,700+seed structures → new allotropes

Mach. Learn.: Sci. Technol. 2026

LEGO-xtal-GPU: differentiable crystal optimization

A fully differentiable pipeline that back-propagates through SO(3)-descriptor objectives and couples them with latent-space refinement, with GPU-parallel batched evaluation. ~5× faster with higher success at meeting target local environments.

  • CVAE
  • SO(3) descriptors
  • GPU batching

Crystal representation in reciprocal space

A physics-informed 4D representation built from Cartesian coordinates and atomic scattering factors, enabling symmetry-aware structure matching and reconstruction and direct comparison with experimental signals.

  • Scattering features
