    F43m at a glance

    F43m (No. 216; full symbol F 4 3 m; Schoenflies symbol Td2) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 43m. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 96 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 43m) to the general position 96i. The symbol is also written F-43m, F43m. Examples: zinc blende ZnS, GaAs; half-Heusler compounds.
