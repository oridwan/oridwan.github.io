    P62c at a glance

    P62c (No. 190; full symbol P 6 2 c; Schoenflies symbol D3h4) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6m2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 32.) to the general position 12i. The symbol is also written P-62c, P62c.
