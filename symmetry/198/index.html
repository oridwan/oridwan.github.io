    P213 at a glance

    P213 (No. 198; full symbol P 21 3; Schoenflies symbol T4) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 23. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry .3.) to the general position 12b. The symbol is also written P213. Examples: B20 compounds FeSi, MnSi; NaClO₃.
