    Ibca at a glance

    Ibca (No. 73; full symbol I 2/b 2/c 2/a; Schoenflies symbol D2h27) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 1) to the general position 16f. The symbol is also written Ibca, I2/b2/c2/a.
