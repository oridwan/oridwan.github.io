    Pmma at a glance

    Pmma (No. 51; full symbol P 21/m 2/m 2/a; Schoenflies symbol D2h5) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry .2/m.) to the general position 8l. The symbol is also written Pmma, P21/m2/m2/a.
