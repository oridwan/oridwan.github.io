    Ia3d at a glance

    Ia3d (No. 230; full symbol I 41/a 3 2/d; Schoenflies symbol Oh10) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3m. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 96 equivalent positions per conventional cell (48 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 16a (site symmetry .3.) to the general position 96h. The symbol is also written Ia-3d, Ia3d, I41/a-32/d. Examples: garnets, e.g. Y₃Al₅O₁₂ (YAG).
