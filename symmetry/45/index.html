    Iba2 at a glance

    Iba2 (No. 45; full symbol I b a 2; Schoenflies symbol C2v21) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mm2. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry ..2) to the general position 8c. The symbol is also written Iba2.
