    Imma at a glance

    Imma (No. 74; full symbol I 2/m 2/m 2/a; Schoenflies symbol D2h28) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 10 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 2/m..) to the general position 16j. The symbol is also written Imma, I2/m2/m2/a.
