    R3m at a glance

    R3m (No. 160; full symbol R 3 m; Schoenflies symbol C3v5) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 3m. Its lattice is rhombohedral (R), described here in hexagonal axes. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 18 equivalent positions per conventional cell (6 for each of the 3 lattice points). There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 3a (site symmetry 3m) to the general position 18c. The symbol is also written R3m.
