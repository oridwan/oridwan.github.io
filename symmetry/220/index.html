    I43d at a glance

    I43d (No. 220; full symbol I 4 3 d; Schoenflies symbol Td6) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 43m. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 48 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 5 Wyckoff positions, from 12a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 48e. The symbol is also written I-43d, I43d.
