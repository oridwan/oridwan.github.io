    Ccce at a glance

    Ccce (No. 68; full symbol C 2/c 2/c 2/e; Schoenflies symbol D2h22) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is C-centred. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 222) to the general position 16i. The symbol is also written Ccce, C2/c2/c2/e.
