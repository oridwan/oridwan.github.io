    I41/a at a glance

    I41/a (No. 88; full symbol I 41/a; Schoenflies symbol C4h6) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/m. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 16f. The symbol is also written I41/a.
