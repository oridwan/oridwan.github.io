    P421c at a glance

    P421c (No. 114; full symbol P 4 21 c; Schoenflies symbol D2d4) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 42m. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 5 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 8e. The symbol is also written P-421c, P421c.
