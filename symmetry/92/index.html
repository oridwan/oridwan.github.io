    P41212 at a glance

    P41212 (No. 92; full symbol P 41 21 2; Schoenflies symbol D44) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 422. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry ..2) to the general position 8b. The symbol is also written P41212.
