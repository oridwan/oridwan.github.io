    I212121 at a glance

    I212121 (No. 24; full symbol I 21 21 21; Schoenflies symbol D29) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class 222. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 2..) to the general position 8d. The symbol is also written I212121.
