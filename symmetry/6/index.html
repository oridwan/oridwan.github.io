    Pm at a glance

    Pm (No. 6; full symbol P 1 m 1; Schoenflies symbol Cs1) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class m. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 2 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry m) to the general position 2c. The symbol is also written Pm, P1m1.
