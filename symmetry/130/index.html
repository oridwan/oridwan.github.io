    P4/ncc at a glance

    P4/ncc (No. 130; full symbol P 4/n 21/c c; Schoenflies symbol D4h8) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 2.22) to the general position 16g. The symbol is also written P4/ncc, P4/n21/cc.
