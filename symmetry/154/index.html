    P3221 at a glance

    P3221 (No. 154; full symbol P 32 2 1; Schoenflies symbol D36) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 32. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 6 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 3a (site symmetry .2.) to the general position 6c. The symbol is also written P3221. Examples: α-quartz SiO₂ (the other enantiomorph).
