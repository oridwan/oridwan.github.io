    R32 at a glance

    R32 (No. 155; full symbol R 3 2; Schoenflies symbol D37) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 32. Its lattice is rhombohedral (R), described here in hexagonal axes. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 18 equivalent positions per conventional cell (6 for each of the 3 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 3a (site symmetry 32) to the general position 18f. The symbol is also written R32.
