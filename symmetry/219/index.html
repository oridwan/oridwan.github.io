    F43c at a glance

    F43c (No. 219; full symbol F 4 3 c; Schoenflies symbol Td5) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 43m. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 96 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 23.) to the general position 96h. The symbol is also written F-43c, F43c.
