    P4/mbm at a glance

    P4/mbm (No. 127; full symbol P 4/m 21/b m; Schoenflies symbol D4h5) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4/m..) to the general position 16l. The symbol is also written P4/mbm, P4/m21/bm.
