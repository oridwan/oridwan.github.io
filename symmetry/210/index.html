    F4132 at a glance

    F4132 (No. 210; full symbol F 41 3 2; Schoenflies symbol O4) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 432. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 96 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 23.) to the general position 96h. The symbol is also written F4132.
