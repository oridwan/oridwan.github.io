    P4 at a glance

    P4 (No. 75; full symbol P 4; Schoenflies symbol C41) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 4d. The symbol is also written P4.
