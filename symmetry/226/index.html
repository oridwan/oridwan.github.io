    Fm3c at a glance

    Fm3c (No. 226; full symbol F 4/m 3 2/c; Schoenflies symbol Oh6) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3m. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 192 equivalent positions per conventional cell (48 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 10 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 432) to the general position 192j. The symbol is also written Fm-3c, Fm3c, F4/m-32/c.
