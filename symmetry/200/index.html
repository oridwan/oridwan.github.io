    Pm3 at a glance

    Pm3 (No. 200; full symbol P 2/m 3; Schoenflies symbol Th1) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 24 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry m3.) to the general position 24l. The symbol is also written Pm-3, Pm3, P2/m-3.
