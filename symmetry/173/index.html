    P63 at a glance

    P63 (No. 173; full symbol P 63; Schoenflies symbol C66) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 6 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 3..) to the general position 6c. The symbol is also written P63.
