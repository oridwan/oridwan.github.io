    P6222 at a glance

    P6222 (No. 180; full symbol P 62 2 2; Schoenflies symbol D64) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 622. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 11 Wyckoff positions, from 3a (site symmetry 222) to the general position 12k. The symbol is also written P6222.
