    Immm at a glance

    Immm (No. 71; full symbol I 2/m 2/m 2/m; Schoenflies symbol D2h25) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 15 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry mmm) to the general position 16o. The symbol is also written Immm, I2/m2/m2/m.
