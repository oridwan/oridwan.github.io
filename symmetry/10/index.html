    P2/m at a glance

    P2/m (No. 10; full symbol P 1 2/m 1; Schoenflies symbol C2h1) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class 2/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 15 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 2/m) to the general position 4o. The symbol is also written P2/m, P12/m1.
