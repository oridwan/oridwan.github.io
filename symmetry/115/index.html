    P4m2 at a glance

    P4m2 (No. 115; full symbol P 4 m 2; Schoenflies symbol D2d5) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 42m. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 4m2) to the general position 8l. The symbol is also written P-4m2, P4m2.
