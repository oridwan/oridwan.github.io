    P222 at a glance

    P222 (No. 16; full symbol P 2 2 2; Schoenflies symbol D21) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class 222. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 21 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 222) to the general position 4u. The symbol is also written P222.
