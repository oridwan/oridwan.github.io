    Aea2 at a glance

    Aea2 (No. 41; full symbol A e a 2; Schoenflies symbol C2v17) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mm2. Its lattice is A-centred. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry ..2) to the general position 8b. The symbol is also written Aea2.
