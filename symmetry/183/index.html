    P6mm at a glance

    P6mm (No. 183; full symbol P 6 m m; Schoenflies symbol C6v1) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6mm. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 6mm) to the general position 12f. The symbol is also written P6mm.
