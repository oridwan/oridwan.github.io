    P42m at a glance

    P42m (No. 111; full symbol P 4 2 m; Schoenflies symbol D2d1) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 42m. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 15 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 42m) to the general position 8o. The symbol is also written P-42m, P42m.
