    P6/m at a glance

    P6/m (No. 175; full symbol P 6/m; Schoenflies symbol C6h1) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 6/m..) to the general position 12l. The symbol is also written P6/m.
