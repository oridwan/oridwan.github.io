    Cmc21 at a glance

    Cmc21 (No. 36; full symbol C m c 21; Schoenflies symbol C2v12) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mm2. Its lattice is C-centred. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry m..) to the general position 8b. The symbol is also written Cmc21.
