    P31m at a glance

    P31m (No. 157; full symbol P 3 1 m; Schoenflies symbol C3v2) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 3m. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 6 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 3.m) to the general position 6d. The symbol is also written P31m.
