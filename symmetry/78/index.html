    P43 at a glance

    P43 (No. 78; full symbol P 43; Schoenflies symbol C44) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. It has a single Wyckoff position, the general position 4a. The symbol is also written P43.
