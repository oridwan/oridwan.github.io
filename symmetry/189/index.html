    P62m at a glance

    P62m (No. 189; full symbol P 6 2 m; Schoenflies symbol D3h3) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6m2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 62m) to the general position 12l. The symbol is also written P-62m, P62m.
