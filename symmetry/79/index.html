    I4 at a glance

    I4 (No. 79; full symbol I 4; Schoenflies symbol C45) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 8c. The symbol is also written I4.
