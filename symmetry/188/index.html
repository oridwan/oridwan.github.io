    P6c2 at a glance

    P6c2 (No. 188; full symbol P 6 c 2; Schoenflies symbol D3h2) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6m2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 3.2) to the general position 12l. The symbol is also written P-6c2, P6c2.
