    P4132 at a glance

    P4132 (No. 213; full symbol P 41 3 2; Schoenflies symbol O7) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 432. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 24 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 5 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry .32) to the general position 24e. The symbol is also written P4132. Examples: β-manganese.
