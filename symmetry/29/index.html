    Pca21 at a glance

    Pca21 (No. 29; full symbol P c a 21; Schoenflies symbol C2v5) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mm2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. It has a single Wyckoff position, the general position 4a. The symbol is also written Pca21.
