    Cmce at a glance

    Cmce (No. 64; full symbol C 2/m 2/c 21/e; Schoenflies symbol D2h18) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mmm. Its lattice is C-centred. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 2/m..) to the general position 16g. The symbol is also written Cmce, C2/m2/c21/e. Examples: black phosphorus; α-gallium.
