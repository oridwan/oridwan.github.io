    Fd3m at a glance

    Fd3m (No. 227; full symbol F 41/d 3 2/m; Schoenflies symbol Oh7) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3m. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 192 equivalent positions per conventional cell (48 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 43m) to the general position 192i. The symbol is also written Fd-3m, Fd3m, F41/d-32/m. Examples: diamond, silicon, germanium; spinel MgAl₂O₄; pyrochlores.
