    P2 at a glance

    P2 (No. 3; full symbol P 1 2 1; Schoenflies symbol C21) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class 2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 2 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 5 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 2) to the general position 2e. The symbol is also written P2, P121.
