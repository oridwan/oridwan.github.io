    P312 at a glance

    P312 (No. 149; full symbol P 3 1 2; Schoenflies symbol D31) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 32. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 6 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 3.2) to the general position 6l. The symbol is also written P312.
