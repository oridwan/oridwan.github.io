    P42/nmc at a glance

    P42/nmc (No. 137; full symbol P 42/n 21/m 2/c; Schoenflies symbol D4h15) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4m2) to the general position 16h. The symbol is also written P42/nmc, P42/n21/m2/c.
