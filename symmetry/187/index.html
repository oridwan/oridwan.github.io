    P6m2 at a glance

    P6m2 (No. 187; full symbol P 6 m 2; Schoenflies symbol D3h1) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6m2. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 15 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 6m2) to the general position 12o. The symbol is also written P-6m2, P6m2. Examples: tungsten carbide WC.
