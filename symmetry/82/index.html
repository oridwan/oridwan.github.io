    I4 at a glance

    I4 (No. 82; full symbol I 4; Schoenflies symbol S42) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 8g. The symbol is also written I-4, I4.
