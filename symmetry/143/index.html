    P3 at a glance

    P3 (No. 143; full symbol P 3; Schoenflies symbol C31) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 3. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 3 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 3..) to the general position 3d. The symbol is also written P3.
