    P4/m at a glance

    P4/m (No. 83; full symbol P 4/m; Schoenflies symbol C4h1) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 4/m..) to the general position 8l. The symbol is also written P4/m.
