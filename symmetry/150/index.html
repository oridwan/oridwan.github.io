    P321 at a glance

    P321 (No. 150; full symbol P 3 2 1; Schoenflies symbol D32) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 32. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 6 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 32.) to the general position 6g. The symbol is also written P321.
