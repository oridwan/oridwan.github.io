    R3c at a glance

    R3c (No. 167; full symbol R 3 2/c; Schoenflies symbol D3d6) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 3m. Its lattice is rhombohedral (R), described here in hexagonal axes. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 36 equivalent positions per conventional cell (12 for each of the 3 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 6a (site symmetry 32) to the general position 36f. The symbol is also written R-3c, R3c, R-32/c. Examples: corundum Al₂O₃; hematite Fe₂O₃; calcite CaCO₃.
