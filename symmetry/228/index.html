    Fd3c at a glance

    Fd3c (No. 228; full symbol F 41/d 3 2/c; Schoenflies symbol Oh8) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3m. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 192 equivalent positions per conventional cell (48 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 16a (site symmetry 23.) to the general position 192h. The symbol is also written Fd-3c, Fd3c, F41/d-32/c.
