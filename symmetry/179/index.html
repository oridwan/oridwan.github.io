    P6522 at a glance

    P6522 (No. 179; full symbol P 65 2 2; Schoenflies symbol D63) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 622. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 12 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 3 Wyckoff positions, from 6a (site symmetry .2.) to the general position 12c. The symbol is also written P6522.
