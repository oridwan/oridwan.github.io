    Fd3 at a glance

    Fd3 (No. 203; full symbol F 2/d 3; Schoenflies symbol Th4) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 96 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 23.) to the general position 96g. The symbol is also written Fd-3, Fd3, F2/d-3.
