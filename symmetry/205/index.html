    Pa3 at a glance

    Pa3 (No. 205; full symbol P 21/a 3; Schoenflies symbol Th6) is a cubic space group in the crystal class m3. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 24 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry .3.) to the general position 24d. The symbol is also written Pa-3, Pa3, P21/a-3. Examples: pyrite FeS₂.
