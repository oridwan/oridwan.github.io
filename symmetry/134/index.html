    P42/nnm at a glance

    P42/nnm (No. 134; full symbol P 42/n 2/n 2/m; Schoenflies symbol D4h12) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 14 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 42m) to the general position 16n. The symbol is also written P42/nnm, P42/n2/n2/m.
