    I41cd at a glance

    I41cd (No. 110; full symbol I 41 c d; Schoenflies symbol C4v12) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4mm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 2..) to the general position 16b. The symbol is also written I41cd.
