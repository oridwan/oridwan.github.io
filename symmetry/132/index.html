    P42/mcm at a glance

    P42/mcm (No. 132; full symbol P 42/m 2/c 2/m; Schoenflies symbol D4h10) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 16 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry m.mm) to the general position 16p. The symbol is also written P42/mcm, P42/m2/c2/m.
