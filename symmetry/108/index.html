    I4cm at a glance

    I4cm (No. 108; full symbol I 4 c m; Schoenflies symbol C4v10) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4mm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 4 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 16d. The symbol is also written I4cm.
