    I41/acd at a glance

    I41/acd (No. 142; full symbol I 41/a 2/c 2/d; Schoenflies symbol D4h20) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/mmm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 32 equivalent positions per conventional cell (16 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 32g. The symbol is also written I41/acd, I41/a2/c2/d.
