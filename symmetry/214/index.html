    I4132 at a glance

    I4132 (No. 214; full symbol I 41 3 2; Schoenflies symbol O8) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 432. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 48 equivalent positions per conventional cell (24 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 8a (site symmetry .32) to the general position 48i. The symbol is also written I4132.
