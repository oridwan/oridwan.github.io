    C2/c at a glance

    C2/c (No. 15; full symbol C 1 2/c 1; Schoenflies symbol C2h6) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class 2/m. Its lattice is C-centred. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 1) to the general position 8f. The symbol is also written C2/c, C12/c1. Examples: many molecular crystals; diopside CaMgSi₂O₆; muscovite mica.
