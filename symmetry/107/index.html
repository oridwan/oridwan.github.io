    I4mm at a glance

    I4mm (No. 107; full symbol I 4 m m; Schoenflies symbol C4v9) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4mm. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 5 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4mm) to the general position 16e. The symbol is also written I4mm.
