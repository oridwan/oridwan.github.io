    P1 at a glance

    P1 (No. 2; full symbol P 1; Schoenflies symbol Ci1) is a triclinic space group in the crystal class 1. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 2 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 9 Wyckoff positions, from 1a (site symmetry 1) to the general position 2i. The symbol is also written P-1, P1. Examples: many molecular crystals; kyanite Al₂SiO₅.
