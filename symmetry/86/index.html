    P42/n at a glance

    P42/n (No. 86; full symbol P 42/n; Schoenflies symbol C4h4) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 4..) to the general position 8g. The symbol is also written P42/n.
