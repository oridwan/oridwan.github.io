    F23 at a glance

    F23 (No. 196; full symbol F 2 3; Schoenflies symbol T2) is a cubic space group in the crystal class 23. Its lattice is face-centred (F). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 48 equivalent positions per conventional cell (12 for each of the 4 lattice points). There are 8 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 23.) to the general position 48h. The symbol is also written F23.
