    P1 at a glance

    P1 (No. 1; full symbol P 1; Schoenflies symbol C11) is a triclinic space group in the crystal class 1. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule, and it is polar. A general point has 1 equivalent positions per conventional cell. It has a single Wyckoff position, the general position 1a. The symbol is also written P1.
