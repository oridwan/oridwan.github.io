    R3 at a glance

    R3 (No. 148; full symbol R 3; Schoenflies symbol C3i2) is a trigonal space group in the crystal class 3. Its lattice is rhombohedral (R), described here in hexagonal axes. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 18 equivalent positions per conventional cell (6 for each of the 3 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 3a (site symmetry 3.) to the general position 18f. The symbol is also written R-3, R3. Examples: ilmenite FeTiO₃; dolomite CaMg(CO₃)₂.
