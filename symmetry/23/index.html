    I222 at a glance

    I222 (No. 23; full symbol I 2 2 2; Schoenflies symbol D28) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class 222. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 11 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 222) to the general position 8k. The symbol is also written I222.
