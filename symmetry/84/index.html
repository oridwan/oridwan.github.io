    P42/m at a glance

    P42/m (No. 84; full symbol P 42/m; Schoenflies symbol C4h2) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 4/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 11 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 2/m..) to the general position 8k. The symbol is also written P42/m.
