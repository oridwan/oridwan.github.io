    I4122 at a glance

    I4122 (No. 98; full symbol I 41 2 2; Schoenflies symbol D410) is a tetragonal space group in the crystal class 422. Its lattice is body-centred (I). It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 16 equivalent positions per conventional cell (8 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 4a (site symmetry 2.22) to the general position 16g. The symbol is also written I4122.
