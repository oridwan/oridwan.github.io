    P212121 at a glance

    P212121 (No. 19; full symbol P 21 21 21; Schoenflies symbol D24) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class 222. Its lattice is primitive. It is non-centrosymmetric and a Sohncke group: it contains no inversion, mirror or glide operations, so it can hold a single enantiomer of a chiral molecule. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. It has a single Wyckoff position, the general position 4a. The symbol is also written P212121. Examples: most common group for chiral molecules and proteins.
