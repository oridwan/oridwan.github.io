    P2/c at a glance

    P2/c (No. 13; full symbol P 1 2/c 1; Schoenflies symbol C2h4) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class 2/m. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 7 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 1) to the general position 4g. The symbol is also written P2/c, P12/c1.
