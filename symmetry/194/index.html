    P63/mmc at a glance

    P63/mmc (No. 194; full symbol P 63/m 2/m 2/c; Schoenflies symbol D6h4) is a hexagonal space group in the crystal class 6/mmm. Its lattice is primitive. It is centrosymmetric. A general point has 24 equivalent positions per conventional cell. There are 12 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry 3m.) to the general position 24l. The symbol is also written P63/mmc, P63/m2/m2/c. Examples: graphite; hexagonal BN; NiAs; 2H-MoS₂; hcp metals Mg, Ti, Zn.
