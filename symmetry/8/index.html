    Cm at a glance

    Cm (No. 8; full symbol C 1 m 1; Schoenflies symbol Cs3) is a monoclinic space group in the crystal class m. Its lattice is C-centred. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 4 equivalent positions per conventional cell (2 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 2 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry m) to the general position 4b. The symbol is also written Cm, C1m1.
