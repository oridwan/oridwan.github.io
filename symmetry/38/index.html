    Amm2 at a glance

    Amm2 (No. 38; full symbol A m m 2; Schoenflies symbol C2v14) is a orthorhombic space group in the crystal class mm2. Its lattice is A-centred. It is non-centrosymmetric, and it is polar. A general point has 8 equivalent positions per conventional cell (4 for each of the 2 lattice points). There are 6 Wyckoff positions, from 2a (site symmetry mm2) to the general position 8f. The symbol is also written Amm2. Examples: orthorhombic BaTiO₃.
