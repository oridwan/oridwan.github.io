Density Functional Theory and VASP

An interactive course from the Schrödinger equation to calculations you can defend.

Density functional theory (DFT) is the method behind most electronic-structure calculations of real materials, and VASP is one of its most widely used codes. This course builds both from first principles: the quantum mechanics you need, the Hohenberg–Kohn and Kohn–Sham theory, exchange–correlation functionals, k-points, plane waves and PAW, and then the calculations used in research, from relaxations and magnetism to phonons, migration barriers and machine-learning potentials.

How the lectures work

Slides with the lecture underneath

Each lecture is a set of 16:9 slides, and under each slide are the notes of what is said about it. Read the page like an article, or press P to present the slides full screen.

Interactive figures

Change a parameter and watch the physics respond. Every number in a figure is computed in your browser; model systems are labelled as models.

Self-contained

Every symbol is defined where it appears and the key equations are derived, with worked numbers, so you can learn without another book open. Terms and VASP tags open a short explanation in place.

Who this course is for

Students and researchers who want to understand what a DFT calculation does, not only how to run one. You need calculus and first-year physics; quantum mechanics is taught from the start. You need access to VASP only for the hands-on labs.

Also on this site: an interactive guide to crystallographic symmetry and the 230 space groups, the language behind POSCAR files and k-point meshes.

How the course is checked

  • Every claim is checked against primary papers, textbooks, lecture notes and the official VASP documentation before it is published. Each lecture ends with a short list of further reading.
  • VASP tags and defaults are checked against the VASP Wiki when a lecture is written.
  • Numbers in interactive figures come from code tested against exact results.
  • Lab results come from real VASP runs, never invented output.
  • Each lecture is fact-checked independently before it is published.
