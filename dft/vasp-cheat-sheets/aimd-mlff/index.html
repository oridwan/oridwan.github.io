VASP AIMD and machine-learned force fields

Molecular dynamics with VASP: choosing the thermostat and ensemble, the defaults that silently give NVE, time steps, restarting and analysing a trajectory, and training VASP's own machine-learned force fields during the run.

The short version

  1. MD is IBRION = 0 with NSW steps of POTIM fs; POTIM has no default, and VASP stops without it.
  2. Always set MDALGO and its coupling tag: every thermostat's coupling defaults to “off”, so a run that sets only MDALGO is silently NVE.
  3. The default MDALGO = 0 folds atoms back into the cell, which rules out a mean-squared displacement. Use MDALGO 1–5.
  4. Constant pressure (NpT) only works with Langevin: MDALGO = 3, ISIF = 3.
  5. Use ISYM = 0, a time step of about 1 fs (0.5–0.7 fs with hydrogen), and check the drift of the conserved energy.
  6. VASP's machine-learned force fields (6.3 and later) train on the fly during MD: train, refit, then run.

Restarting and changing ensemble

  • cp CONTCAR POSCAR continues the trajectory: CONTCAR carries the velocities and the integrator's predictor–corrector data.
  • Switching ensemble (NVT to NpT, say): delete the predictor–corrector block at the end of the file, or the run crashes; keep the velocities.
  • Equilibrate first, then count only the production part. Starting velocities come from TEBEG unless POSCAR has them; set RANDOM_SEED for reproducible runs.

Analysis

  • OSZICAR's MD line: T= temperature, E= total energy including kinetic and thermostat terms (the conserved quantity), F= and E0= as in relaxations, EK= kinetic energy, SP= and SK= thermostat energies.
  • In NVE and Nosé–Hoover runs, E must not drift systematically; drift means too large a time step or noisy forces (a poorly converged SCF). Stochastic thermostats do not conserve it exactly.
  • XDATCAR holds a frame every NBLOCK steps; with MDALGO ≥ 1 the coordinates are not folded back into the cell. In ASE read all frames with read("XDATCAR", index=":"); without the index you get only the last one.
  • Diffusion: D from the mean-squared displacement, ⟨|r(t) − r(0)|²⟩ = 2 n D t in n dimensions; pymatgen's DiffusionAnalyzer reads vasprun.xml files directly. Radial distribution: PCDAT (averaged) or ASE's get_rdf. Vibrational DOS: Fourier transform of the velocity autocorrelation (VELOCITY = .TRUE.).
  • MDAnalysis has no VASP reader: convert with ASE first.

Machine-learned force fields in VASP

VASP can learn a force field during MD: it computes a step from first principles only when the model's estimated error is too large, and otherwise predicts.

  • Refit before production: without it the fast prediction mode, 20 to 100 times faster, is not available.
  • Keep the INCAR and POTCAR of the first-principles part identical across training stages; no MAXMIX while training; never train in NVE.
  • Aim for training errors below about 1 meV/atom in energy and 30–100 meV/Å in forces.
  • Surfaces and molecules: a tiny stress weight, ML_WTSIF = 1E-10.
  • A force field describes one potential-energy landscape: for a magnet, train on one consistent magnetic state.
  • Since VASP 6.6.0 a run stops if the model's spilling factor becomes critically large (above 0.9).

Common mistakes

  • No POTIM: VASP stops at once. No coupling tag: a silent NVE run.
  • MDALGO = 0, then a mean-squared displacement from wrapped coordinates.
  • NpT with a thermostat other than Langevin.
  • Too large a time step for hydrogen; or a heavier hydrogen mass (POMASS) when dynamics matter.
  • Symmetry left on (ISYM).
  • A CONTCAR from an NVT run reused for NpT without removing the predictor–corrector block.
  • Reading XDATCAR with ASE without index=":".
  • Spin-polarized r²SCAN MD or force-field training on VASP 6.6.0.
