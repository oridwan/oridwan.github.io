How the sheets are checked

  • Every tag, default and recommendation is read on the VASP Wiki when its sheet is written, and the sheet says which VASP version it was checked against.
  • Every INCAR on a sheet is checked by the same rules as the INCAR checker, and the build rejects unknown or misspelled tags.
  • Shell commands are tested on real VASP output files.
  • Labels separate VASP's own advice from rules of thumb, and say when a setting needs a newer VASP or the VTST add-on.
